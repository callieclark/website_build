Emergency Food Access

This project evaluates emergency food access in New York City from a multi-dimensional perspective. We simulate travel time to the closest food pantry throughout the week and across four different modes of transportation. We partner with City Harvest to incorporate the varied operating hours of each pantry, and then consider the underlying urban transit infrastructure by simulating travel time for walking, biking, driving and public transit. We then propose a framework to construct an Emergency Food Access Index (EFAI), and identify the gaps in service through evaluating areas with high need and low access.
